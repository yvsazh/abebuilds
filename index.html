Before you install

Windows
Unzip the whole folder and run Epic.exe. Keep the files next to it.
macOS
Unzip and move Epic.app to Applications. The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Android
Open the APK on your device and allow installs from this source when asked.
iOS
Open the project in Xcode on a Mac, choose your signing team, then build to your iPhone or iPad.
Browser
Use a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Clearing this site's data also clears your browser save.
